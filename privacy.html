This website

This site is static. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics, and does not track you. Web fonts are served by Google Fonts, which receives your IP address as part of that request.

Our games

Our games are single-player and run offline. Save data stays on your machine. When a game is bought through Steam, Valve handles the purchase, account and achievement data under its own privacy policy; we receive only aggregate, non-identifying statistics.

Email

If you email us, we keep that correspondence for as long as needed to answer it. We do not add you to any mailing list without you asking.
