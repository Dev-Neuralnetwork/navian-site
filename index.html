ABOUT

Navian Digital is an independent games publisher, founded in 2026. It has one studio: Dusk Moth Games, currently building The Offering.

We back small, dense games with hand-made art and systems you can read at a glance — no stock assets, no microtransactions, no live-service treadmill.

Everything ships on desktop. Everything is playable offline.
